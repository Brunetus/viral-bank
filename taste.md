## universal
- **Borrow a premise that is already circulating, as real footage, and answer it with
  your own.** The ceiling post is still compounding five days on: **603,506 views,
  25,358 shares, 1,795 saves, 619 comments** (+76k views since 09-17). A stranger's
  clip states the claim, a hard cut to the poster's own material answers it.
- **The template is not the premise.** The same cut pattern under a self-written line
  ("the hottest thing a guy can do is…", 10 shares on 3.4k views) lands in the ordinary
  band. The recognition lives in a premise the feed is already arguing about.
- **Forwarding is the growth engine, and it shows up in the first hours.** The three
  posts that broke out all had shares running at 20-40 per 1,000 views on day one;
  everything else sits under 3 per 1,000 and stays there.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest holds (8.3-9.2s) forwarded
  1-8 times; the best forwarder held 7.8s. Optimise the hook for reach, the body for hold.
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one.** "Send this to…" closes draw 1-2 shares.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,766 → 3,681 → 3,286 views, 1-3 saves and shares on each.
