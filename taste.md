## universal
- **Borrow a premise that is already circulating, as real footage, and answer it with
  your own.** The ceiling post has nearly doubled in two days, to **455,729 views,
  19,881 shares, 1,339 saves, 495 comments**: a stranger's clip states the claim, a hard
  cut to the poster's own material answers it. The borrowed clip recruits; the owned
  answer is why it gets forwarded.
- **The template is not the premise.** A third run the next day, with a weaker borrowed
  line ("she's not wrong") under a feature tip, drew 3,110 views. Still 35 shares, the
  best forwarding rate of any non-ceiling post, but no breakout. The recognition lives in
  a premise the feed is already arguing about, not in the cut pattern.
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one, and it is not close.** "Send this to
  the person on your team who…" took 1 share. The biggest forwarders asked for nothing.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest hold (11.1s) forwarded 3
  times; the best forwarder ever held 7.8s. Optimise the hook for reach and the body
  for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- **A named choice the viewer can place themselves in beats an open question.** The
  close offering three named tiers is now at 495 comments; two "be honest, [yes/no
  question]?" closes drew 0 each.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,747 → 3,662 → 3,257 views, 1 save and 2 shares on each.
