## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** A 7.0s-hold
  reel was forwarded 26,040 times; an 11.8s-hold reel the same night was forwarded 69 times.
  Optimise for the forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, shows, or DOES, with a real receipt where possible.**
  A real stats screen ("24 billion tokens burnt") did 609k / 26k shares in two days; an abstract
  activity ("building an app") on the same kind of opener did 3.4k / 20.
- **The opener and the object are a PAIR; neither carries alone.** Same opener, two objects: 3.4M
  vs 10.6k. Same object, two openers: 3.6k vs 81k. A strong setup cannot rescue a weak object.
- **A list opener must deliver its list.** Five promised, five delivered: 174k / 8,284 shares.
  Promised a list and paid with one item: 2.3k / 15 shares, 44% skip.
- **A tutorial or numbered setup in the caption is not a hook.** Process reels (stack lists,
  strategy talk, "here is the hack") sit at 0-126 shares with 44-49% skip.
