## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The six widest
  reels this window are all a stranger's premise with one blank in it. The reel that
  merely *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **The blank must be filled by ONE ownable, picturable OBJECT.** Sharpest line in the
  data, six days running. Blanks answered by a single object ran 40k-1.44M views at
  1.4-4.1% shares. Blanks answered by a LIST, a REASON or an abstraction topped out at
  5,069 views and 0.27-0.90%. Same form, same craft, 100x apart.
- **The frame is reusable; only the OBJECT gets spent.** Measured, not inferred: the
  window's #1 premise was re-run with a DIFFERENT object and went 120,679 → 1,311,822 →
  **1,442,198 views** across two days, passing the original it was copied from. A premise
  is not spent when it ships. Keep the question, swap the answer.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The settled top reel carries 32,477
  shares against 29,002 likes: more than one share per like, 20 days out. A cut
  engineered to be *sent to someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Read share RATE late, and never rank on it.** Rate falls as reach widens: the biggest
  reel reads 1.40% because its denominator exploded, while a reel 36x smaller reads 3.11%.
  Shares are the signal; shares ÷ views is a maturity marker, not a score. Give any reel
  48h before reading either.
- **ASK FOR THE ANSWER IN ONE WORD.** New this window and the cheapest craft note here.
  Two reels end on an explicit one-word prompt ("tokens or bank, which one do you check
  first? one word" / "local or cloud? one word"). They are the window's #1 and #2 by
  share RATE among non-wide reels (2.00% and 2.80%) and carry its two lowest skip rates
  among reels that also travelled. Naming the shape of the reply lowers the cost of
  replying.
- **A low skip rate is a RETENTION signal, not a reach signal — but the two can co-occur.**
  Last window's lowest-skip reel (28.3%) finished at 4,212 views and 2 shares, so a held
  audience is not a handed-on one. This window's lowest (27.6%) is also its best young
  share rate. Read skip rate alongside shares, never instead of them.
- **Watch time is the same finding from the other side.** The longest-held reel (15.13s)
  did 3,061 views; the widest did 1.44M at 8.17s, and a 58k-view reel is the SHORTEST
  hold on the account (5.71s). Length holds an audience you already have. A premise with
  a blank is what buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final
  beat. Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.

What works / what dies
- Reels that open on the CHILD and the ordinary problem hold 19-25% skip; reels that open
  on the app or the service hit 56-86%.
- The three reels over 2,000 views this window all name a universal parent moment in the
  first line and only mention the map at the end, if at all.
- Dies: launch/announcement framing, "see bio", anything where the first frame is a screen.
- Zero shares and zero saves across 11 of 12 reels: this account has a retention engine and
  no distribution engine. The blank-premise form that works on @imabgold is untested here.
- Small numbers: treat anything under ~200 views as noise, not a signal.
