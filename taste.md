## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The five widest
  reels this window are all a stranger's premise with one blank in it. The reel that
  merely *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **The blank must be filled by ONE ownable, picturable OBJECT.** Sharpest line in the
  data and it has now held four days running. Blanks answered by a single object ran
  37k-790k views at 1.6-4.1% shares. Blanks answered by a LIST, a REASON or an
  abstraction topped out at 4,617 views and 0.31-0.74%. Same form, same craft, 100x apart.
- **A premise is NOT spent when it ships — it is spent when the object is reused.**
  Correction to the previous read, and it is the biggest finding of this window: the
  window's #1 premise was re-run today with a DIFFERENT single object in the blank and
  went to 120,679 views / 1,958 shares inside a day. What died in earlier re-runs was a
  repeat of the same *answer*, not a repeat of the question. Keep the frame, swap the object.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The top reel carries 31,998 shares
  against 28,492 likes: more than one share per like, 18 days out. A cut engineered
  to be *sent to someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Read share RATE late, never early.** Shares lag views by hours: today's wide reel
  reads 1.62% at a few hours old and is climbing, while settled wide reels sit at
  1.9-4.1% and settled ordinary ones under 0.8%. A low rate on day one means nothing;
  give any reel 48h before calling it.
- **A low skip rate is a RETENTION signal, not a reach signal — never optimise for it.**
  The lowest-skip reel this window (27.9%) finished at 4,179 views; the wide ones sit at
  32-44%. Holding the people who stayed and being handed to new people are different machines.
- **Watch time is the same finding from the other side.** The longest-held reel (15.35s
  average) did 2,927 views; the widest did 790,034 at 7.82s, and the window's #4 by reach
  is the SHORTEST-held reel on the account (5.68s). Length holds an audience you already
  have. A premise with a blank is what buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final
  beat. Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.
