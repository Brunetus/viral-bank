## universal
- **The duplicate-hook tax is now proven twice, and the second run was worse.** The
  same announcement caption posted twice in one day took 2,382 views at 29.5% skip
  on the first run and 375 views at 53.5% on the second — an 84% collapse. The
  earlier pair (31.7% → 60.6% skip) said the same thing. A hook is spent the moment
  it ships. Never re-run one, not the next day and above all not the same day.
- Hold and forwarding are separate engines and the gap keeps widening. The longest
  hold on record (11.2s, 29.5% skip) produced 3 total forwards; the biggest
  forwarding post (64 shares) holds 6.5s; and the single best forward-per-view post
  in this window holds just 5.2s. Optimise the hook for reach, the body for hold,
  and never read one as evidence of the other.
- The strongest forwarding shape on record is a COMPETENCE CONFESSION that ends in
  the competence failing. "Twelve years taught me how to escalate professionally,
  then it shipped me the same wrong answer four times" took 64 shares; "my whole
  career was being the person who says no to spending" took 25 shares on 5,973
  views. Name a credential, then show it break.
- An ANNOUNCEMENT can win hold and still forward nothing. The launch post is the
  longest hold ever recorded here and produced 1 save and 2 shares. People will
  watch your news to the end and not pass it on, because there is nothing in it
  that says something about the sender. Hold is not distribution.
- A short, unhedged EMOTIONAL REACTION to a thing that already happened out-forwards
  the thing itself. A three-word caption over a reaction beat took 23 shares and 113
  likes on 5.2s of hold, against 2 shares for the full announcement it referred to.
  The aftermath is more sendable than the news.
- Self-implication only works as a REVERSAL, not as a mood. The flip from the
  expected subject to the actual one is the mechanism; the same speaker admitting
  the same thing with no turn skips at 60-67%. An admission without a turn is a
  bottom-three hold every time it has been tried.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Showing a
  real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time AND 12 shares.
  A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
