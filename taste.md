## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Name a shared experience in the first line and let the viewer recognise it.
  Both accounts' best reels this window open on a specific thing the viewer has
  already met ("every parent knows this chair", "it wrote a whole game, then it
  asked me for permission"), not on a capability or a claim.
- Confession plus a hard number is still the ceiling. The single widest-reaching
  identity line this window carried a dollar figure in it.
- A claim the footage immediately contradicts buys real watch time: state the
  flattering version out loud, let the frame disprove it, never acknowledge it.
- Telling people what a thing is FOR remains the fastest death on record. Show
  one moment instead; the worst retention ever measured here came from explaining.
- Show-and-tell of a feature underperforms the story of using it. Same subject,
  same week: the story held ~60% longer than the demo.
- Promo cues in the first line still kill ("link in bio", "see bio", "find and
  add" all sit at 57-73% skip). Pitch after the payoff or not at all.
- Length is fine when the story keeps opening. The top reels this window run
  8-9s of watch time; nothing is being punished for not being short.
