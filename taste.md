## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The three biggest
  reels this window are all a stranger's question with one blank in it. The reel that
  merely *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The top reel carries 29,164 shares against
  25,599 likes: more than one share per like, and it is still climbing 9 days out. A cut
  engineered to be *sent to someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Share RATE reads the ceiling early.** Every reel that went wide sat above ~2% shares
  per view inside its first day; every reel that floored sat under 0.5%. You can grade a
  cut at six hours off that one ratio, long before views tell you anything.
- **Watch time and reach are separate machines.** The longest-held reel this window
  (15.5s average) did 2,833 views; the widest did 715,220 at 7.8s. Length holds an
  audience you already have. A premise with a blank is what buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final
  beat. Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.
- **Text beats need reading time.** Reels whose opening card is readable muted hold
  30-40% skip rates; reels whose hook is spoken-only sit at 60-70%.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** Re-running a premise that already worked
  returns a fraction: the same superlative aired twice dropped from 48,017 to 2,459.
