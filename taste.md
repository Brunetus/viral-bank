## universal
- **Borrow a premise that is already circulating, as real footage, and answer it with
  your own.** The ceiling post is still compounding a week on: **643,444 views,
  26,575 shares, 1,937 saves** (+18k views since 09-21). A stranger's
  clip states the claim, a hard cut to the poster's own material answers it.
- **This is now four for four, not one lucky post.** Every reel built on a borrowed
  premise has cleared 29k views and 690 shares; nothing else on the account has passed
  7k. The newest (09-19) is at **61,016 views / 1,661 shares after three days**. The pattern is
  the most reliable thing either account has ever measured.
- **The template is not the premise.** The same cut pattern under a self-written line
  ("the hottest thing a guy can do is…", 10 shares on 3.5k views) lands in the ordinary
  band. The recognition lives in a premise the feed is already arguing about.
- **Forwarding is the growth engine, and it shows up in the first hours.** All four
  breakouts ran 20-42 shares per 1,000 views on day one; everything else sits under 3
  per 1,000 and stays there. Day-one share rate is the only early read that predicts.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest holds (8.2-9.2s) forwarded
  1-8 times; the best forwarder held 7.8s. Optimise the hook for reach, the body for hold.
- **A question aimed straight at the viewer outforwards a statement.** "what do you
  want?" (915 shares in a day) and "what is the equivalent of flowers for you" (693)
  both hand the viewer a line to answer; declarative versions of the same idea did not.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,770 → 3,684 → 3,294 views, 1-3 saves and shares on each.
