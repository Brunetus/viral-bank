## universal
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one, and it is not close.** A post that
  ended "send this to the person on your team who…" has 1 share on 3,305 views. A
  post that asked for nothing and handed over three techniques the viewer could use
  has **91 shares on 6,290**, and it was still climbing three days after posting
  (87 saves+shares yesterday, 104 today). Portable things keep travelling after the
  reach curve has flattened. Requests do not.
- **Saves and shares answer different questions.** "I will need this later" saves;
  "you need to see this" shares. A one-line tool tip took 6 saves on 3,017 views,
  the second-highest save count in the window, while its shares stayed at 4. Pick
  which one the post is for before writing it.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest hold on record (11.1s)
  forwarded 4 times; the best forwarder (104) is a middle hold at 5.9s. Optimise the
  hook for reach, the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. A real
  screen on camera took 8.3s and 12 shares. A real timecode as proof forwarded 13
  times but held only 5.7s: once the number is read, the viewer has what they came for.
- **An ADMISSION LEFT OPEN is the only thing that has ever moved comments.** Naming a
  gap in your own knowledge and refusing to close it took 8 comments on an account
  that otherwise runs zero. It needs an unclosed hole, not a mood.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption shipped three times in a single
  day went 3,694 → 3,468 → 2,176 views, with 0 comments on all three.
