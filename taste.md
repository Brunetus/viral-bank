Refreshed 2026-10-29 (25 imabgold reels, 25 bambi reels, full insights incl. skip rate on all 50).

## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** A 6.4s-hold
  reel was forwarded 2,467 times; reels holding 8.6s were forwarded 15-249 times. Optimise for the
  forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object. Held on BOTH accounts: the family account's two best recent
  reels are blank-shaped ("It isn't the snacks… It's one question"; "You get ______.").
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, shows, or DOES with a tool, not an adjective.**
  Named, picturable objects and habits he can confess to carry; a real receipt (a stats screen, a
  checkout, a config file) counts.
- **The opener and the object are a PAIR; neither carries alone.** Same object, two openers: 3.5k
  vs 57k, 77k vs 3.0M. Same opener, two objects: 3.0M vs 5.8k. A strong setup can revive a spent
  object, and it cannot rescue a weak one.
- **A list opener must deliver its list.** Five promised, five delivered: 161k / 7,609 shares.
  Promised a list and paid with one item: 2.3k / 15 shares, 44% skip.
- **Never correct the viewer, and never explain your own strategy to him.** Corrections and
  "here is how I did it" reels were forwarded 0-19 times, with the worst skip rates in the set.

What works
- A girl-to-camera superlative/desire opener ("the sexiest thing a man can do", "what a woman looks
  for in a man", "he's not single because") + ONE ownable object + a one-word vote.
- A real screen or file as the object (/stats, a commit graph, .env, a rig). The proof IS the payoff.
- An object he can DO today (.env, ship to main) beats one he BUYS (Max plan) or MONITORS (tokens).
