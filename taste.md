## universal
- Hold and forwarding are separate engines and the gap is now measured twice. The
  longest hold ever recorded here (11.0s, 28.9% skip) produced 3 total forwards;
  the biggest forwarding post (64 shares) holds 6.5s. Optimise the hook for reach,
  the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- The strongest forwarding shape on record is a COMPETENCE CONFESSION that ends in
  the competence failing, and it has now replicated. "Twelve years taught me how to
  escalate professionally, then it shipped me the same wrong answer four times"
  took 64 shares; "my whole career was being the person who says no to spending"
  took 25 shares and 5,966 views. Name a credential, then show it break.
- An ANNOUNCEMENT can win hold and still forward nothing. The launch post held the
  audience longer than anything ever posted here and produced 1 save. People will
  watch your news to the end and not pass it on, because there is nothing in it
  that says something about the sender. Hold is not distribution.
- Self-implication only works as a REVERSAL, not as a mood. The flip from the
  expected subject to the actual one is the mechanism; the same speaker admitting
  the same thing with no turn skips at 60-67%. An admission without a turn is a
  bottom-three hold every single time it has been tried.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Showing a
  real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time AND 12
  shares. A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
- An unresolved outcome forwards; a label does not. Short "let's see how long this
  lasts" posts with a visible stake carry a quarter of lifetime shares, while the
  same in-joke with nothing at risk is a bottom-three hold.
- Never re-run a hook inside 24 hours. A caption repeated the same day lost 67% of
  its views and doubled its skip rate (31.6% → 60.6%).
