## universal
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one, and it is not close.** A post that
  ended with an explicit "send this to the person on your team who…" took **0 shares
  on 3,214 views** — while holding 8.1s at 29.8% skip, a top-three hold. A post that
  asked for nothing and instead handed over three techniques the viewer could use
  took **75 shares on 5,704**. Asking for the forward does not produce it. Giving
  the viewer something portable does.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines and can point opposite ways.** The
  longest hold on record (11.1s) forwarded 3 times. The best forwarder on record
  (87 saves+shares) is a MIDDLE hold at 5.9s and a 33% skip. Optimise the hook for
  reach, the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- **The ceiling shape is ANTI-CREDENTIAL + STEAL-THIS.** "People assume I do this
  the skilled way. I don't — here are the three crude things that actually work."
  87 saves+shares, roughly 3x anything else, and it beat the previous record holder
  (a competence confession that ends in the competence failing, 70) using the same
  move plus a takeaway. Confess the gap, then make the gap useful.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Pointing a
  camera at a real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time
  AND 12 shares. A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
- **An ADMISSION LEFT OPEN is the only thing that has ever moved comments.** A post
  naming a gap in the speaker's own knowledge and refusing to close it took 8
  comments on an account that otherwise runs zero. Self-implication as a mood does
  nothing; it needs either a reversal (for hold) or an unclosed hole (for comments).
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** The same caption re-run 24h later took
  4.2s hold at 60.6% skip against 6.1s at 31.8% for run one. Never re-run a hook.
