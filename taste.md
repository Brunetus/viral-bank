Refreshed 2026-10-30 (25 imabgold reels, 25 bambi reels, full insights incl. skip rate on all 50).

## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** A 7.0s-hold
  reel was forwarded 13,463 times; a 10.5s-hold reel the same night was forwarded 25 times.
  Optimise for the forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, shows, or DOES, with a real receipt where possible.**
  A real stats screen ("24 billion tokens burnt") did 322k / 13k shares in a day; an abstract
  activity ("building an app") on the same kind of opener did 3.3k / 19.
- **The opener and the object are a PAIR; neither carries alone.** Same opener, two objects: 3.2M
  vs 9.5k. Same object, two openers: 3.6k vs 79k. A strong setup cannot rescue a weak object.
- **A list opener must deliver its list.** Five promised, five delivered: 168k / 7,957 shares.
  Promised a list and paid with one item: 2.3k / 15 shares, 44% skip.
- **A tutorial or numbered setup in the caption is not a hook.** Process reels (stack lists,
  strategy talk, "here is the hack") sit at 0-112 shares with 45-77% skip.

What works
- A girl-to-camera superlative/desire opener ("the sexiest thing a man can do", "he's not single
  because", "what a woman looks for in a man") + ONE ownable object + a one-line vote/number CTA.
- A real screen or file as the object (/stats with a big number, .env, a commit graph, a rig).
- An object he can DO or SHOW today beats one he BUYS (Max plan) or vaguely does ("build an app").
What dies
- Corrections ("he's doing it wrong"), tutorials, stack lists, founder strategy: 0-112 shares.
- A weak donor on a proven object (`De9aGDYo6rb` 3.6k; .com x6 at 2-24k).
- A list-promise opener paid with one item (`De7-bnPCvtX`, 2,333 / 44% skip).
- A premise aimed at a sub-group ("engineering students", 10k / 21) or a stat about himself.
