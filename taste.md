## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** Today's
  set makes the point harder than any before it: the best-held reel ever measured here
  (`DegDVfaJWIJ`, 17.5s hold) was forwarded 8 times to 1,341 people. The third-worst hold in the
  set (5.7s, `DdxZCCmR8i0`) was forwarded 1,369 times to 64,902. Optimise for the forward.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k in this
  window opens on a claim plus its eliminations ("not money. not power.") and withholds the
  object. Every reel under 5k answers itself in the first sentence.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, not a behaviour.** Named, picturable objects
  carry; adjectives and habits do not. Object COUNT is irrelevant — a one-object payoff and a
  three-object payoff land in the same share band.
- **A frame is reusable; only the object is spent.** One sentence, re-run with a fresh object five
  weeks apart: 853k, then 1.81M, then 136k. Rebuild the winner, swap the noun.
- **Never correct the viewer.** The zero-share reels in the set both tell him he is doing it wrong
  ("10 terminals is not how you run Claude Code", "every frontier model is available"). They held
  9.9s and 5.4s and were forwarded 0 times. A correction is not forwardable; a compliment he can
  prove about himself is.
- **A question earns reach only when it is aimed AT the viewer.** "i have a question for the men.
  what do you want?" → 4,380 shares. "do you wanna know why he's not texting you right now?" →
  10 shares. Same grammar, different addressee.
- **The tail is the asset.** The window's biggest reel is still accruing thirteen days out
  (+17,328 views and +228 shares in the last 24h). A forward chain outlives the post.
- **A how-to can forward if the hero is the viewer's setup, not the viewer's error.** The best
  non-premise reel of the last ten days (`DedNsnBNWSh`, 101 shares) describes a thing he can copy;
  the worst (`DeTHQuuukIh`, 0 shares) describes a thing he does wrong. Same subject, same length.
