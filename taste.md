## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Identity-confession hooks out-reach announcements. "I am X and I have never
  done Y" travels; "here is the thing I made" does not.
- Reach is bought with shares and saves, not likes. The reels that got sent to
  someone else had a concrete, surprising story beat, not the nicest footage.
- Watch time and reach are different prizes. This window the widest-reaching
  reels held the SHORTEST average watch (4.5-5.2s) while the longest holds
  (9-11s) barely got distributed. Short and punchy earns the push.
- Promo framing kills retention inside the first second: "link in bio",
  "mapped", "find and add" all sat at 60-72% skip. A pitch loses the viewer
  before the hook lands.
- A question asked in the first line and answered at the end holds people to
  the payoff. Open the loop, close it, in that order.
- Named specifics beat generic aesthetics. A real place, a real number, a real
  failure outperforms beautiful footage with a vague caption.
