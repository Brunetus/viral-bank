## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Skip rate in the first seconds is the reach lever, not watch time. Across
  both accounts every reel that cleared 1.4k views had skip < 34%; every reel
  above 37% skip stalled under 1.3k. Win the first second or nothing
  downstream matters.
- High watch-time with high skip is depth, not reach: the longest-watched
  reel of the month reached 262 people at 52% skip. A few people watching a
  lot is a different outcome than many watching a little.
- A concrete first line beats an announcement. Sensory specifics ("the bibs
  were for us, not the baby") earn the second; category framing ("find X
  anywhere in the world") gets swiped.
- Product-pitch and CTA reels die. The pitch can live in the caption; the
  video has to be a story.
- Re-running a hook that once won decays hard — the novelty was the asset,
  not the wording. Rotate the angle, don't recycle the sentence.
- Payoff LAST, and give text beats time to read: front-loading the reveal
  kills the rewatch.
