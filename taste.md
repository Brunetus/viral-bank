Derived data, full overwrite each scan. History lives in git. Read with catalog.md.
Refreshed 2026-10-24 (25 imabgold reels, 25 bambi reels, full insights incl. skip rate on all 50).

## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** The
  best-held reel in the set (15.5s) was forwarded 9 times; a 5.8s-hold reel was forwarded 1,417
  times. Optimise for the forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object. Every reel under 5k answers itself in the first sentence.
  Held on BOTH accounts: the family account's two best recent reels are blank-shaped ("It isn't
  the snacks… It's one question"; "The baby gets the good chair. You get ______.").
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns or can show, not a behaviour.** Named, picturable
  objects carry; adjectives and habits do not. A real receipt (a stats screen, a checkout) counts.
- **A frame is reusable, but the object has to be worth the frame.** The winner's exact frame
  rebuilt with a smaller noun (a domain upgrade) did 6.5k in two days against the original's 1.9M.
  The frame earns the open; the object earns the forward.
- **Never correct the viewer, and never explain your own strategy to him.** Corrections and
  "here is how I did it" reels held 6-10s and were forwarded 0-3 times.
- **The tail is the asset.** Hits keep accruing for weeks; a forward chain outlives the post.
