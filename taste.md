## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Identity-confession hooks out-reach announcements, and the confession gets
  stronger when it carries a hard number: "I ran a $35M company and could not do
  X" travels further than "I could not do X."
- A concrete strange-thing-happened story is the single most reliable reach
  engine. The best performers this window all narrate one specific event with a
  turn in it, not a capability or a feeling.
- Watch time and reach stopped trading against each other. The top reel this
  window held 8.3s AND reached the widest — the old "short and punchy or bust"
  rule is not holding. Length is fine when the story keeps opening.
- Abstract framing of a thing's purpose is the fastest death known: the worst
  retention on record (88% skip) came from explaining the idea instead of showing
  a moment.
- Promo cues in the first line still kill: "link in bio", "mapped", "find and
  add" sit at 60-73% skip. Pitch after the payoff or not at all.
- Open a question in the first line and answer it at the end. Loop closed, in
  that order, is what holds people to the last frame.
