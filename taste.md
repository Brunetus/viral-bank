  "let's see how long this lasts" posts with a visible clock hold 59% of one
  feed's lifetime shares, while the same in-joke with no clock is a bottom-three
  hold. The format is not the asset, the unresolved outcome is.

  lost 67% of its views and its skip rate went 31.5% → 60.8%. A hook is spent
  once the audience has met it; the second run is not a second chance.
- Never make the product itself the subject. Milestone and how-to-use-it posts
  hold the three worst skip rates on record (86.3%, 75.5%, 72.9%), across two
  unrelated accounts.
