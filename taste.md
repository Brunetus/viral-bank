## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** A 9.8s hold
  was forwarded 24,893 times; another 9.8s hold was forwarded twice. Optimise for the forward.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 100k opens on a
  claim or question and withholds the object.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, shows, or DOES, with a real receipt where possible.**
  Same opener: a concrete file setup did 126k / 4,648 shares; an abstract activity ("building an
  app") did 2.5k / 11.
- **The opener and the object are a PAIR; neither carries alone.** The same object ($200 API
  credit) did 54k / 1,792 and 40k / 1,234 on two openers and 2.2k / 4 on a third.
- **A list opener must deliver its list.** Promised a list and paid with one item: 2.3k / 15 shares.
- **A tutorial or a pitch in the opener is not a hook.** Process and offer reels sit at 2-130 shares.
