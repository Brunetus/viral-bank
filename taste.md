## universal
- **The duplicate-hook tax is proven twice and neither run recovered.** The same
  announcement caption shipped twice in one day took 2,396 views at 29.6% skip on
  the first run and 440 at 52.6% on the second, an 82% collapse that has not closed
  in two days of tail. The earlier pair (31.8% → 60.7% skip) said the same thing. A
  hook is spent the moment it ships. Never re-run one, least of all the same day.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines, and this window proves they can point
  opposite ways.** The second-best forwarder here (27 saves+shares) is a
  BOTTOM-THREE hold at 5.2s, while the longest hold on record (11.1s, 29.6% skip)
  produced 3 forwards total. An announcement can win hold and forward nothing:
  people will watch your news to the end and not pass it on, because there is
  nothing in it that says something about the sender. Hold is not distribution.
  Optimise the hook for reach, the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of
  the other.
- The strongest forwarding shape on record is a COMPETENCE CONFESSION that ends in
  the competence failing. "Twelve years taught me how to escalate professionally,
  then it shipped me the same wrong answer four times" is at 69 saves+shares and
  still climbing; "my whole career was being the person who says no to spending"
  took 25 shares on 5,975 views. Name a credential, then show it break.
- A short, unhedged EMOTIONAL REACTION to a thing that already happened
  out-forwards the thing itself. A three-word caption over a reaction beat took 24
  shares on 5.2s of hold, against 2 shares for the full announcement it referred
  to. The aftermath is more sendable than the news.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Showing a
  real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time AND 12 shares.
  A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
- Self-implication only works as a REVERSAL, not as a mood. The flip from the
  expected subject to the actual one is the mechanism; the same speaker admitting
  the same thing with no turn skips at 60-67%. An admission without a turn is a
  bottom-three hold every time it has been tried.
