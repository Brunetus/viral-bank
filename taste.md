## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Skip rate in the opening second is the reach lever. Every reel this window
  that cleared 1.4k views held skip under 34%; every reel above 40% skip
  stalled under 1k, whatever its watch time. Win second one or nothing
  downstream matters.
- Long watch with high skip is depth, not reach: the longest-watched reel of
  the set (11.4s) reached 572 people at 36.9% skip. A few people watching a
  lot is a different outcome from many watching a little.
- A concrete first line beats an announcement. Sensory specifics ("the bibs
  were for us, not the baby", 29.5% skip) earn the second; category framing
  ("find X anywhere in the world", 72.5% skip) gets swiped.
- Story in the video, payload in the caption. The reels topping saves+shares
  pair a narrative clip with a numbered list underneath. A video that IS the
  list gets skipped and saved by nobody.
- Product pitches and CTAs die on camera. The pitch can live in the caption;
  the video has to be a story.
- Re-running a hook that once won decays fast and takes skip rate with it.
  The novelty was the asset, not the wording: rotate the angle, keep the
  identity, retire the sentence.
- Payoff LAST, and give text beats time to read: front-loading the reveal
  kills the rewatch.
