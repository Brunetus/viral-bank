## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The seven widest
  reels this window are all a stranger's premise with one blank in it. The reel that merely
  *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **The blank must be closed by a PICTURABLE, OWNABLE OBJECT, and the count is NOT what
  matters. CONFIRMED AT 72h, after nine scans claiming the opposite.** The three-object
  payoff that was the natural test went 9,262 views → 99,963 → **120,869 views / 2,993
  shares** across three days, still climbing. A second three-object reel sits at 129,772 /
  2.87%. What actually dies is a blank closed by a BEHAVIOUR, a REASON, or a deferral: "the
  3 rules are in the video" (5,393 views, 0.82%), "how I know he's selfless" (4,113, 0.27%),
  "things men say when they're lying" (4,770, 0.48%). One object or three, name them and
  show them in frame. The moment the answer is something to understand rather than something
  to own, it stops travelling.
- **ASK FOR THE ANSWER IN ONE WORD.** The reel ending on an explicit one-word prompt ("local
  or cloud? one word") holds the account's highest share rate at **4.95% (34,786 views /
  1,723 shares)** and its lowest skip (29.4%) — up again from 4.84% yesterday. It carries no
  borrowed opener and no stranger's premise: own footage plus a named reply shape beats every
  donor-led reel in the window on rate. Naming the shape of the reply lowers the cost of
  replying, and the cost of replying is what distribution is priced on.
- **The frame is reusable; only the OBJECT gets spent.** Measured over five days: the
  window's #1 premise was re-run with a DIFFERENT object and has reached **1,615,623 views /
  22,355 shares**, past the original it was copied from on views, reach and saves, and still
  +48,766 views in the last 24h. A premise is not spent when it ships. Keep the question,
  swap the answer.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The settled top reel carries 33,127 shares
  against 29,737 likes: more than one share per like, three weeks out. A cut engineered to be
  *sent to someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Read share RATE late, and never rank on it.** Rate moves hard for 72h (this window's
  young reel went 1.45% → 2.58% → 2.48% while views went up 13x) and then falls as reach
  widens (the 1.6M reel reads 1.38% because its denominator exploded). Shares are the signal;
  shares ÷ views is a maturity marker. Give any reel 48–72h before you believe its rate.
- **Watch time is the same finding from the other side.** The longest-held reel (14.96s) did
  4,113 views; the widest did 1.6M at 8.17s, and a 61k-view reel is the SHORTEST hold on the
  account (5.73s). Length holds an audience you already have. A premise with a blank is what
  buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final beat.
  Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.
