## universal
- **Borrow a premise that is already circulating, as real footage, and answer it with
  your own.** It has now set the ceiling twice in five days, the second time by 10x:
  a stranger's clip states the claim, a hard cut to the poster's own material answers
  it. **235,540 views, 10,706 shares, 665 saves.** The borrowed clip does the
  recruiting; the owned footage is why it gets forwarded.
- **The wording of a premise is not the premise.** The same "she said / he's not
  texting you" framing reused as the poster's own opening line drew 876 and 701 views
  the same weekend. Hypothesis (from captions, not frames): recognition comes from
  having seen the circulating thing before, not from its sentence shape.
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one, and it is not close.** "Send this to
  the person on your team who…" took 1 share. The two biggest forwarders asked for
  nothing.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest hold (11.1s) forwarded 4
  times; the best forwarder ever (10,706) held 7.9s. Optimise the hook for reach and
  the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- **An open question buys nothing; a named choice the viewer can place themselves in
  does.** "Which plan are you on? pro, max, or still on the free one?" drew 195
  comments. "Be honest, who do you talk to more?" drew 0, and an open question on a
  34k-view post drew 1.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,740 → 3,648 → 3,244 views, 0 comments on all three.
