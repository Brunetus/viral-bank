  your own.** The ceiling post is still compounding ten days on: **661,082 views,

  premise has cleared 30k views and 720 shares; nothing else on the account has passed
  7k. The 09-19 post is at **68,147 views / 1,855 shares on day four** (+7.1k overnight).
- **The template is not the premise.** The same cut pattern under a self-written line
  ("the hottest thing a guy can do is…", 10 shares on 3.6k) lands in the ordinary band.
  The recognition lives in a premise the feed is already arguing about.
- **The premise has to describe the viewer, not just ask them something.** Today's
  post borrows a question shape but aims it at nobody in particular ("when was the last
  time you felt loved?") and is running **6.2 shares per 1,000 in its first hours** —
  above the dead band, nowhere near the 24-41 per 1,000 all four breakouts ran on day
  one. A question is the delivery; being the person described is the payload.
- **Forwarding is the growth engine and it shows up in the first hours.** Day-one share
  rate is the only early read that predicts: 24-41 per 1,000 → breakout, under 5 → the
  ordinary band, and nothing has ever climbed out of the second group later.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest holds (8.1-9.1s) forwarded
  1-8 times; the best forwarder held 7.8s. Optimise the hook for reach, the body for hold.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,776 → 3,694 → 3,303 views, 1-3 saves and shares on each.
