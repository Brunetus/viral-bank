## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The five widest
  reels this window are all a stranger's question with one blank in it. The reel that
  merely *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **The blank must be filled by ONE ownable, picturable OBJECT.** This is the sharpest
  line in the data and it now cuts both ways. Blanks answered by a single object went
  36k-757k views. Blanks answered by a LIST ("things men do that are attractive"),
  by a REASON ("how I know men are selfless") or by an abstraction ("when it gets real")
  finished at 626-4,491 with share rates of 0.00-0.67%. Same form, same craft, 100x apart.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The top reel carries 30,764 shares
  against 26,000-odd likes: more than one share per like, 16 days out. A cut engineered
  to be *sent to someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Share RATE reads the ceiling on day one, and it keeps holding.** Everything that went
  wide sits at 1.9-4.1% shares per view; everything that floored sits under 0.8%, and the
  gap is visible within hours. One ratio, one morning, before views say anything.
- **A low skip rate is a RETENTION signal, not a reach signal — never optimise for it.**
  The three lowest-skip reels this window (27.7%, 29.5%, 33.4%) finished at 2,892-4,349
  views; the five widest sat at 34-44% skip. Holding the people who stayed and being
  handed to new people are different machines.
- **Watch time is the same finding from the other side.** The longest-held reel (15.5s
  average) did 2,892 views; the widest did 757,191 at 7.8s. Length holds an audience you
  already have. A premise with a blank is what buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final
  beat. Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** Re-running a premise that already worked
  returns a fraction: the same superlative aired twice dropped from 48,732 to 2,487.
