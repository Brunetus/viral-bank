  your own.** The ceiling post is still compounding at day ten: **675,352 views,
  27,783 shares, 2,045 saves** (+14.3k views and +506 shares in 24h). A stranger's clip
  states the claim, a hard cut to the poster's own material answers it.

  premise has cleared 30k views and 770 shares; nothing else on the account has passed
  5.6k. The 09-19 post is at **76,282 views / 2,097 shares / 124 comments on day five**
  (+8.1k views overnight) and is the only one besides the ceiling that pulls comments.
- **The template is not the premise.** The same cut pattern under a self-written line
  ("the hottest thing a guy can do is…") has now been run three times and landed at
  3,609 / 702 / 401 views. The recognition lives in a premise the feed is already
  arguing about, not in the edit.
- **The premise has to describe the viewer, not just ask them something.** The 09-23
  post borrows a question shape but aims it at nobody in particular ("when was the last
  time you felt loved?"): it doubled overnight to 10.0 shares per 1,000, well above the
  dead band and still 2.5x under the weakest breakout. A question is the delivery;
  being the person described is the payload.
- **Forwarding is the growth engine and the early read is directional, not final.**
  Day-one share rate sorts hard: 24-41 per 1,000 → breakout, under 5 → ordinary. The
  middle moves (6.2 → 10.0 per 1,000 in a day) but has never crossed into the top band.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines.** The longest hold in the window (10.1s,
  33.9% skip) forwarded 10 times; the best forwarder held 7.8s. Optimise the hook for
  reach, the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,776 → 3,694 → 3,303 views, with 1-3 saves and shares on each.
