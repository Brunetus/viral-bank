Derived data, full overwrite each scan. History lives in git. Read with catalog.md.
Refreshed 2026-10-18 (25 imabgold reels, 25 bambi reels, full insights incl. skip rate on all 50).

## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** The
  best-held reel in the set (16.4s) was forwarded 8 times; a 5.7s-hold reel was forwarded 1,383
  times. Optimise for the forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object. Every reel under 5k answers itself in the first sentence.
  Now held on BOTH accounts: the family account's best restart reel is "It isn't the snacks. It
  isn't going early. It's one question", the same claim-eliminations-blank shape.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns or can show, not a behaviour.** Named, picturable
  objects carry; adjectives and habits do not. A real receipt (a stats screen, a checkout) counts.
- **A frame is reusable; only the object is spent.** Rebuild the winner, swap the noun.
- **Never correct the viewer.** Reels that tell him he is doing it wrong held 5-10s and were
  forwarded 0 times. A compliment he can prove about himself is forwardable; a correction is not.
- **A mock-negative frame can forward if the "flaw" is a flex.** "How X are still going broke"
  is grammatically a complaint and functionally a boast; the viewer forwards it as self-description.
- **The tail is the asset.** Hits keep accruing for weeks; a forward chain outlives the post.
