## universal
- **A question the viewer wants to answer out-loud beats a statement.** The four biggest
  reels this window are all a stranger's question with one blank in it. The reel that
  merely *describes* the same idea sits 100x lower. Leave the blank; do not close it.
- **Shares, not likes, are the ceiling metric.** The top reel carries 29,544 shares against
  26,044 likes: more than one share per like, 14 days out. A cut engineered to be *sent to
  someone* outperforms a cut engineered to be admired.
- **Share RATE reads the ceiling early, and it holds up.** A reel graded at 2.26% shares
  per view at six hours finished its first 30 hours at 49,940 views / 892 shares. Every
  reel that went wide sat above ~1.5% inside its first day; every reel that floored sat
  under 0.5%. One ratio, six hours, before views say anything.
- **Watch time and reach are separate machines.** The longest-held reel this window
  (15.5s average) did 2,863 views; the widest did 726,117 at 7.8s. Length holds an
  audience you already have. A premise with a blank is what buys a new one.
- **Payoff last, and only once.** Every high-share reel lands its object in the final
  beat. Every low one answers itself in the first third, then keeps talking.
- **Text beats need reading time.** Reels whose opening card is readable muted hold
  33-43% skip rates; reels whose hook is spoken-only sit at 60-70%.
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** Re-running a premise that already worked
  returns a fraction: the same superlative aired twice dropped from 48,732 to 2,487.

What works / what dies
- A borrowed woman-to-camera opener with an open blank, cut at 3.5-4.6s, then his own
  footage as the answer. That one shape is all four hits and nothing else is close.
- The object that fills the blank must be OWNABLE and picturable: a whiteboard, a token
  counter, a 20x plan. "A moment", "a feeling", "when it gets real" all floored.
- Eliminations raise the ceiling ("not money, not power" / "not abs, not a car" / "isn't
  money or looks").
- Dies: announcements, model-release news, travel observation, anything where his own
  line closes the loop before the last beat.
- Dies: a premise the account has already aired. Both "hottest thing a guy can do" cuts
  (2,487 and 3,652) ran a theme the 48.7k reel had already spent.
