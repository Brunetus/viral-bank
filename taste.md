## universal
- **ANSWERING a premise the audience is already arguing about beats introducing one,
  and this window it beat it by 7x.** The ceiling post opened on somebody else's
  claim — a line already circulating, quoted flat — then spent its runtime answering
  it with the poster's own material: **854 shares and 111 saves on 34,418 views**,
  against a previous record of 104 saves+shares. The borrowed premise did the
  recruiting; the owned footage did the proving.
- **An unrequested share beats a requested one, and it is not close.** A post ending
  "send this to the person on your team who…" took 1 share on 3,363 views. The two
  biggest forwarders in the window asked for nothing.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines, and the gap is now enormous.** The
  longest hold on record (11.1s) forwarded 3 times. The best forwarder ever recorded
  here (854) held 6.6s, below the window's own average. Optimise the hook for reach
  and the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- **A direct question does not buy comments. An unclosed hole does.** The 34k-view
  post ended on a plain question and got ONE comment. The only posts that have ever
  moved comments (8, twice) name a gap in the poster's own knowledge and refuse to
  close it — a file never opened, a verdict left hanging. Being visibly unresolved is
  the mechanism; asking is not.
- **Deflate your own contribution and it travels; describe your own process and it
  dies.** "I stood there holding a marker, contributing nothing" is the ceiling.
  "The key is 1" and "the exact order, in 22 seconds" are the floor (1 and 0
  saves+shares, 60.1% skip on the second).
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships.** One caption run three times in a day went
  3,729 → 3,639 → 3,231 views, 0 comments on all three.
