## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Name a shared experience in the first line and let the viewer recognise it. The
  widest reach in this window came from lines the viewer had already lived, never
  from a capability or a claim.
- A claim the footage immediately contradicts buys real watch time: state the
  flattering version out loud, let the frame disprove it, never acknowledge it.
- Watch time and sharing are two different products. The reels that held longest
  this window were quiet confessions almost nobody passed on; the most-shared reel
  ran mid-pack on retention. Decide which one a cut is for before shaping the hook.
- A caption that leaves something for the viewer to answer is the cheapest unpulled
  lever there is. The one reel in this window that provoked a real comment thread
  did it by admitting an unresolved feeling, not by asking a question.
- Telling people what a thing is FOR remains the fastest death on record, and
  showing the feature is only slightly better than explaining it. Show one moment
  of using it instead.
- Promo cues in the first line still kill ("link in bio", "see bio", "find and
  add" all sit at 57-73% skip). Pitch after the payoff or not at all.
- Length is fine when the story keeps opening. The top reels here run 8-9s of
  watch time. Nothing is being punished for not being short.
