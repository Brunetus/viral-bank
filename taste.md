## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, and watch time is not correlated with it.** The
  best-held reel in the set (14.4s) was forwarded 21 times; a 6.3s-hold reel 2,231 times. Optimise
  for the forward, not the hold.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 40k opens on a
  claim and withholds the object. Held on BOTH accounts: the family account's two best recent
  reels are blank-shaped ("It isn't the snacks… It's one question"; "You get ______.").
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, shows, or DOES with a tool, not an adjective.**
  Named, picturable objects and habits he can confess to carry; a real receipt (a stats screen, a
  checkout, a config file) counts.
- **A frame is reusable, the object mostly gets spent.** One object reused six times did 1.9-23k
  each. The exception so far: an object whose first use was a how-to came back once under a fresh
  donor and travelled again (52k / 1,414 shares in a day).
- **A list opener must deliver its list.** Five promised, five delivered: 136k / 6,196 shares.
  Promised a list and paid with one item: 2.2k / 10 shares, 44% skip.
- **Never correct the viewer, and never explain your own strategy to him.** Corrections and
  "here is how I did it" reels were forwarded 0-3 times, with the worst skip rates in the set.

What works
- A mock-confession of a dev habit, or a premise + one ownable object, closed by a one-word or
  one-number vote (main 1.68M / 66k; hottest-things 136k / 6.2k).
- A real screen or file as the object (/stats, a commit graph, .env). The proof IS the payoff.
- Second person or "men" as the addressee; a CTA that asks for HIS number/object/one word.
- A girl-to-camera "what a woman looks for in a man" opener on a fresh-ish object (.env 52k).

  texting you").
