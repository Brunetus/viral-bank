## universal
- **Share COUNT is the distribution lever, not watch time.** The widest reel this window holds
  8.2s average watch; the longest hold in the whole set (14.9s) reached 2,670 people. The two
  numbers move in opposite directions here. A reel that is forwarded travels; a reel that is
  merely finished does not.
- **A premise with one blank in it travels; a statement does not.** Every reel over 100k this
  window opens on a claim plus its eliminations ("not money. not power.") and withholds the
  object. Every reel under 5k answers itself in the first sentence.
- **The payoff must be a thing the viewer owns, not a behaviour.** Named, picturable objects
  carry; adjectives and habits do not. Object COUNT is irrelevant — a one-object payoff and a
  three-object payoff land in the same share band.
- **A frame is reusable; only the object is spent.** The same premise re-run with a fresh object
  did 837k and then 1.72M, five weeks apart: 2.5M views off one sentence. Rebuild the winner,
  swap the noun.
- **Open on the problem, never on the product.** The lowest-reach reels in both accounts open on
  a thing to go get; the highest open on a situation the viewer is already in.
- **Skip rate reads the first second, and it is the cheapest number to act on.** A first frame
  that is a face mid-sentence holds; a first frame that is a screen, a logo or a title card is
  where the audience leaves.
- **Reach does not decay on a wide reel the way the dashboard implies.** The top reel added
  ~27,000 views and 356 shares on day eight, still climbing. Judge a reel's ceiling at day 10,
  never at day 1.
