## universal
- **A hook is spent the moment it ships, and the tax is now measured twice.** The
  same caption re-run 24h later took 4.2s hold at 60.7% skip against 6.1s at 31.8%
  for run one; the same-day repeat pair ran 2,410 views at 29.6% skip then 443 at
  52.6%. Neither run recovered on the tail. Never re-run a hook.
- **Hold and forwarding are separate engines and can point opposite ways.** The
  longest hold on record (11.1s, 29.6% skip) forwarded 3 times; the window's
  second-best forwarder (29 saves+shares) is a BOTTOM-THREE hold at 5.2s. People
  will watch your news to the end and still not pass it on, because there is nothing
  in it that says something about the sender. Optimise the hook for reach, the body
  for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- The strongest forwarding shape on record is a COMPETENCE CONFESSION that ends in
  the competence failing — a named credential, then shown breaking. It sits at 70
  saves+shares and is still climbing five days out, roughly 3x anything else here.
- A short, unhedged EMOTIONAL REACTION to a thing that already happened
  out-forwards the thing itself: a three-word caption over a reaction beat took 26
  shares, against 3 for the full announcement it referred to. The aftermath is more
  sendable than the news.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Pointing a
  camera at a real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time
  AND 12 shares. A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
- **An ADMISSION LEFT OPEN is the only thing that has ever moved comments.** A post
  naming a gap in the speaker's own knowledge and refusing to close it took 8
  comments on an account that otherwise runs zero, and held them through a second
  day. Self-implication as a mood does nothing; it needs either a reversal (for
  hold) or an unclosed hole (for comments).
