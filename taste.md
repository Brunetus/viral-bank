## universal
- Reach and hold are separate engines and a post can win one while losing the
  other. The record-reach post (8,029 views, 61 shares) holds 6.5s; the longest
  hold in the same window (8.7s) reached 1,849. Optimise the hook for reach and
  the body for hold, and never read one as evidence of the other.
- The strongest forwarding shape on record is a COMPETENCE CONFESSION that ends
  in the competence failing. "Twelve years taught me how to escalate
  professionally. Then it shipped me the same wrong answer four times" is the
  biggest post ever recorded here: 61 shares, 31.1% skip. Naming a credential
  and then showing it break is a reach move, not just a hold move.
- Self-implication only works as a REVERSAL, not as a mood. The flip from the
  expected subject to the actual one is the mechanism; the same speaker admitting
  the same thing with no turn skips at 60-67%. An admission without a turn is a
  bottom-three hold every single time it has been tried.
- Evidence a viewer can check travels; a claim about yourself only holds. Showing
  a real screen and saying the thing on it is real took 8.3s watch time AND 12
  shares. A verifiable artifact is the cheapest share trigger there is.
- An unresolved outcome forwards; a label does not. Short "let's see how long
  this lasts" posts with a visible stake carry the majority of lifetime shares,
  while the same in-joke with nothing at risk is a bottom-three hold.
- A post ABOUT the work getting made does not forward. Gear announcements,
  milestone posts and "here is how the system works" all land mid-reach with
  zero saves and zero shares. The output is the post; the process is not.
- Never re-run a hook inside 24 hours. A caption repeated the same day lost 67%
  of its views and doubled its skip rate (31.6% → 60.7%).
