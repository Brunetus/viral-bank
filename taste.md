## universal
Account-stripped craft — the ONLY section customer jobs (JOBS.md) may read.
- Skip rate in the first seconds is the reach lever, not watch time. Every
  reel across both accounts that cleared 1.4k views held skip under 34%;
  every reel above 37% skip stalled under 1.3k. Win second one or nothing
  downstream matters.
- Long watch with high skip is depth, not reach: the longest-watched reel of
  the set reached 262 people at 52% skip. A few people watching a lot is a
  different outcome from many watching a little.
- A concrete first line beats an announcement. Sensory specifics ("the bibs
  were for us, not the baby") earn the second; category framing ("find X
  anywhere in the world") gets swiped.
- Story in the video, payload in the caption. The reels that top saves+shares
  pair a narrative clip with a numbered list underneath. A video that IS the
  list gets skipped.
- Product pitches and CTAs die on camera. The pitch can live in the caption;
  the video has to be a story.
- Re-running a hook that once won decays hard, and it decays fast. The
  novelty was the asset, not the wording: rotate the angle, keep the identity,
  retire the sentence.
- Payoff LAST, and give text beats time to read: front-loading the reveal
  kills the rewatch.
