# Viral playbook — the July 2026 meta

Audio-model tag per entry: **diegetic (bakeable)** = the clip/format carries its
own audio, fine to keep in the MP4 · **trending sound (add in-app)** = a licensed
IG-library track; never bake it in, select it natively when posting from the phone
(business accounts get muted/throttled for uploaded copyrighted audio) · **flexible**
= works either way.

Banked footage exists for a concept only where noted `→ clips/`.

## General canon (works for any account or customer job)

**1. Saxophone-gets-louder chaos zoom** · audio: trending sound (add in-app)
Sax blares to full volume as the camera hard-zooms/freezes on something mundane
going wrong. 80s-movie melodrama over a trivial stake.
Splice: any "and then it all broke" beat. User's real fail is the payoff; the sax is the drama.

**2. Wipe-the-camera reveal** · audio: trending sound (add in-app)
Hand wipes the lens, and on the wipe the scene cuts to the reveal. Cleanest
product/location transition on Reels right now (origin @james.and.antler).
Splice: a user's "here's the thing itself" reveal. Setup wipe borrowed, payoff is theirs.

**3. Sound-impression: confidence vs. reality gap** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
People confidently make the SOUND of a thing to camera, then it cuts to the actual
thing with their audio over it. Comedy lives in the gap.
Splice: "what people think X sounds like vs. what it is" — a build, a workflow, a baby.

**4. Headphone swap** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
Two people swap headphones and react to each other's music. Humor from clashing taste, zero production.
Splice: any "reacting to what the other person is into" contrast bit.

**5. "POV: telling someone to cross the street in 2026"** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
POV framing, one person plays a whole scene straight to camera. Curiosity-gap hook that's sayable before you record.
Splice: a user POV mini-scene setup; the payoff is their punchline.

**6. Truck-driver deadpan lip-sync** · audio: trending sound (add in-app) · → clips/ (DZ4PL1dNUlF, DZwiNBXNqII)
Single person, low-production, mouthing a song completely deadpan. The format IS the hook (@cio0061, 7.4M likes).
Splice: a deadpan "me, unbothered, while X" reaction over a user's chaos. BANKED — see catalog.md.

**26. "Meet my [X], they are very [trait]" — the footage instantly disproves the claim** · audio: diegetic (bakeable) · 🔥 LIVE (2026-08-06) ·scan · → clips/ DbjAfeTsSCh, Dbr6mRMJewR
Held text makes a flat, confident claim about a subject who is visibly doing the opposite in the same frame, and nobody ever acknowledges it. The tension is then paid off by escalating the contradiction all the way to an extreme close-up reveal, not by a written punchline.
Splice: claim the thing the user is transparently bad at ("I am very disciplined about stopping at 6pm", "she is very calm about restaurants"), then let their own footage be the contradiction and cut hard to the close-up. Re-sighted 2026-08-06 in a past-tense variant that is cheaper to shoot: instead of a present-tense trait, the text reports a REFUSAL that already happened ("POV: your dad said he didn't want a puppy") while the evidence sits in frame the whole time — no escalation and no close-up needed, the subject just declines to acknowledge it and smiles. Use this version when the user has a real "they swore they were against this" reversal. (sources: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbjAfeTsSCh/ · https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dbr6mRMJewR/)

**13. "Dear Instagram, please connect me with [X]" — algorithm-plea caption** · audio: flexible · CAPTION FORMAT, not a banked clip · 🔥 LIVE (2026-07-19)
A direct plea to the algorithm over aspirational b-roll: "Dear instagram, please do your thing and connect me with [startup obsessed people who aren't afraid to start at 0]". Engagement-bait / manifestation framing that invites comments and openly signals the exact audience you want to reach.
Use: an imabgold "connect me with other people building with Claude / non-technical founders shipping real things" version over the user's OWN build/founder footage — the caption is the hook, the user's material is the hero and the b-roll. Drives COMMENTS more than watch-time (a saves/shares + reach play). Recreate the format with your own footage; don't repost someone else's clip. (Sourced from @aarondambruso; the footage was cut from the bank as ad-adjacent — the caption is the viral asset.)

**22. Role-labelled news dramatisation (two-hander or ensemble)** · audio: diegetic (bakeable) · 🔥 LIVE (2026-07-30) ·scan · → clips/ DbHFRLzJjuH, DbTsR5LR5W3
A real headline is pinned as a card up top, then two performers act it out wearing floating on-screen labels for who they are ("AI" / "AI companies"), one of them playing the powerful party as a dopey child. No punchline is written — the actual facts of the story are the punchline, and a naive "yay that sounds cool and fun" is the button.
Splice: label yourself and the thing you're fighting, act out this week's real AI news, and let the user's own build/screen be what the joke is defended against. Works solo too (film both roles, swap the label). Re-sighted 2026-07-30 in an ensemble variant: a whole conference table of people in paper company badges passing the same money in a circle under a Bloomberg chyron, so the label can be a worn prop rather than a floating graphic. (sources: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbHFRLzJjuH/ · https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbTsR5LR5W3/)

**24. "POV: the [inanimate object]" personification** · audio: trending sound (add in-app) · 🔥 LIVE (2026-07-30) ·scan · → clips/ DbTc4IRhNDT
A human physically climbs into the object's situation and performs its entire ordeal in character, held text naming it ("POV: the fitted sheet in the dryer"). Then a hard reality-cut to the actual object alone is the button.
The joke lives in the commitment to the bit and the deflation of that cut, not in any line.
Splice: name the object that quietly suffers in the user's real routine (the laptop at 3am, the stroller in an airport, the highchair at the end of a meal), perform its ordeal, then reality-cut to the user's own footage of the real thing. (source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbTc4IRhNDT/)

**28. Walk-and-talk explainer with headline screenshots as receipts** · audio: diegetic (bakeable) · FORMAT ONLY, footage unusable · 🔥 LIVE (2026-08-06) ·scan · → clips/ DbqpAqORnm8 (DO-NOT-SPLICE, record only)
One narrator walks a city street straight through the whole piece, never stopping, while the screen is papered with real headline screenshots (WSJ, NYT, Bloomberg) pinned over each claim as proof, plus a reaction meme dropped on the punchline of every second line. The walking is what makes two minutes of finance explainer watchable — the camera never sits still and the receipts do the credibility work so the narrator can stay funny.
Splice: an imabgold "here is what actually happened this week in AI" walk, with real Anthropic/OpenAI headlines pinned as receipts and the user's own build screen as the payoff shot at the end. The hero material is the user talking and their real screen; the headlines are set dressing. Note it is a long-form format — taste.md says length is fine now when the story keeps opening, but only with a turn every few seconds. (source: https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbqpAqORnm8/)
