# Viral playbook — the July 2026 meta

Audio-model tag per entry: **diegetic (bakeable)** = the clip/format carries its
own audio, fine to keep in the MP4 · **trending sound (add in-app)** = a licensed
IG-library track; never bake it in, select it natively when posting from the phone
(business accounts get muted/throttled for uploaded copyrighted audio) · **flexible**
= works either way.

Banked footage exists for a concept only where noted `→ clips/`.

## General canon (works for any account or customer job)

**1. Saxophone-gets-louder chaos zoom** · audio: trending sound (add in-app)
Sax blares to full volume as the camera hard-zooms/freezes on something mundane
going wrong. 80s-movie melodrama over a trivial stake.
Splice: any "and then it all broke" beat. User's real fail is the payoff; the sax is the drama.

**2. Wipe-the-camera reveal** · audio: trending sound (add in-app)
Hand wipes the lens, and on the wipe the scene cuts to the reveal. Cleanest
product/location transition on Reels right now (origin @james.and.antler).
Splice: a user's "here's the thing itself" reveal. Setup wipe borrowed, payoff is theirs.

**3. Sound-impression: confidence vs. reality gap** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
People confidently make the SOUND of a thing to camera, then it cuts to the actual
thing with their audio over it. Comedy lives in the gap.
Splice: "what people think X sounds like vs. what it is" — a build, a workflow, a baby.

**4. Headphone swap** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
Two people swap headphones and react to each other's music. Humor from clashing taste, zero production.
Splice: any "reacting to what the other person is into" contrast bit.

**5. "POV: telling someone to cross the street in 2026"** · audio: diegetic (bakeable)
POV framing, one person plays a whole scene straight to camera. Curiosity-gap hook that's sayable before you record.
Splice: a user POV mini-scene setup; the payoff is their punchline.

**6. Truck-driver deadpan lip-sync** · audio: trending sound (add in-app) · → clips/ (DZ4PL1dNUlF, DZwiNBXNqII)
